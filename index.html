A revised fifth edition

Keep what works.
Strengthen what does not.

5th Edge keeps the familiar rhythm of fifth-edition play while revisiting class progression, species options, spell access, and character-building rules. The goal is not to bury the game under new systems. It is to make the choices already at the table feel better.
