What is 5th Edge?

A revision, not a replacement.

5th Edge keeps the recognizable structure of fifth edition while tightening progression, improving weak options, and giving classes more of their identity earlier.

The website is being built as the actual rules reference, not a marketing shell around a separate document. The same data is intended to power the character builder and rules pages.
