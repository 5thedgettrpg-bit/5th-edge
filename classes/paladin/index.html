Class Features

Hit Points1st level+
Hit Dice: 1d10 per paladin level
Hit Points at 1st Level: 10 + your Constitution modifier
Hit Points at Higher Levels: 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier per paladin level after 1st
Proficiencies1st level+
Armor: All armor, shields
Weapons: Simple weapons, martial weapons
Tools: None
Saving Throws: Wisdom, Charisma
Skills: Choose two from Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Persuasion, and Religion.
Lay on Hands1st level+

Your blessed touch can heal wounds. You have a pool of healing power that replenishes when you finish a long rest. The pool contains hit points equal to five times your Paladin level.

As a bonus action, you can touch a creature and draw power from the pool to restore hit points, up to the amount remaining in the pool.

Alternatively, you can expend 5 hit points from the pool to cure one disease or neutralize one poison affecting the target. This feature has no effect on Constructs or Undead.

Sacred Oath1st level+

At 1st level, you swear the oath that binds you as a paladin forever. Your choice grants you features at 1st level, and again at 3rd, 6th, 11th, 17th, and 20th level.

Oath Spells. Each oath has associated spells. Once you gain access to an oath spell, you always have it prepared, and it does not count against the number of spells you can prepare. If an oath spell is not normally a Paladin spell, it counts as a Paladin spell for you.

Channel Divinity. Your oath allows you to channel divine energy to fuel magical effects. You can use Channel Divinity once, and regain the use when you finish a short or long rest. If an option requires a saving throw, the DC equals your Paladin spell save DC.

Spellcasting1st level+

Charisma is your spellcasting ability for your Paladin spells.

Spell save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier =

Spell attack modifier = proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier =

You prepare a number of Paladin spells equal to your Charisma modifier + half your Paladin level, rounded down, minimum one. Oath Spells and other granted spells do not count against this number.

Fighting Style2nd level+
At 2nd level, you adopt a particular style of fighting as your specialty. You can't take the same Fighting Style more than once.
Divine Smite2nd level+

Starting at 2nd level, when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can expend one spell slot to deal radiant damage to the target in addition to the weapon's damage.

The extra damage is 2d8 for a 1st-level spell slot, plus 1d8 for each spell level higher than 1st, to a maximum of 5d8. The damage increases by 1d8 if the target is a Fiend or Undead.

Warrior of Divinity3rd level+

At 3rd level, your divine presence shields you from harm.

You always have the Shield spell prepared, and it does not count against the number of Paladin spells you can prepare.

You can cast Shield without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all expended uses when you finish a long rest. You can also cast Shield normally using your spell slots.

Harness Divine Power7th level+

At 7th level, you can expend your use of Channel Divinity to fuel your spellcasting. As a bonus action, touch your holy symbol, utter a prayer, and regain one expended spell slot.

The slot can be no higher than half your proficiency bonus, rounded up.

Ability Score Improvement4th level+
At 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1. You can't increase an ability above 20 this way. If feats are allowed, you can take a feat instead.
Extra Attack5th level+
Beginning at 5th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.
Aura of Protection6th level+
Starting at 6th level, whenever you or a friendly creature within 10 feet of you makes a saving throw, the creature gains a bonus equal to your Charisma modifier, minimum +1. You must be conscious to grant this bonus. At 18th level, the range increases to 30 feet.
Divine Health9th level+
By 9th level, the divine magic flowing through you makes you immune to disease.
Aura of Courage10th level+
Starting at 10th level, you and friendly creatures within 10 feet of you can't be frightened while you are conscious. At 18th level, the range increases to 30 feet.
Improved Divine Smite11th level+
By 11th level, you are so suffused with divine power that whenever you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, the creature takes an extra 1d8 radiant damage. This damage is in addition to any Divine Smite damage.
Blessed Restoration13th level+

Beginning at 13th level, your healing magic leaves a lingering divine blessing.

When you restore hit points to a creature using Lay on Hands or a Paladin spell, that creature gains the effects of Bless until the start of your next turn. This effect requires no concentration and does not stack with the Bless spell or another use of this feature.

Cleansing Touch14th level+
Beginning at 14th level, you can use your action to end one spell affecting yourself or one willing creature you touch. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier, minimum once, and regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
Divine Mastery15th level+
Starting at 15th level, you can use your Channel Divinity twice between rests instead of once. You regain both uses whenever you normally regain Channel Divinity.
Aura Improvements18th level+
At 18th level, the range of your Paladin auras increases from 10 feet to 30 feet. This includes Aura of Protection, Aura of Courage, and any Sacred Oath feature that specifically improves when your Paladin aura range increases.
