Class Features

Hit Points1st level+
Hit Dice: 1d10 per ranger level
Hit Points at 1st Level: 10 + your Constitution modifier
Hit Points at Higher Levels: 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier per ranger level after 1st
Proficiencies1st level+
Armor: Light armor, medium armor, shields
Weapons: Simple weapons, martial weapons
Tools: None
Saving Throws: Strength, Dexterity
Skills: Choose two from Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Investigation, Nature, Perception, Stealth, and Survival.
Expertise: Choose one skill you are already proficient in from any source.
Hunter's Instinct1st level+

At 1st level, your training, awareness, and experience have sharpened your instincts, allowing you to react when skill alone is not enough. When you attack with a weapon you are proficient with, you can use your Wisdom modifier, instead of Strength or Dexterity, for the attack and damage rolls.

You have a number of Instinct Dice equal to your proficiency bonus. Your Instinct Die is a d4.

When you make an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can expend one Instinct Die, roll it, and add the number rolled to the result.

You can choose to use your Instinct Die after you roll the d20, but before the DM determines whether the roll succeeds or fails.

You can expend only one Instinct Die per roll.

You regain all expended Instinct Dice when you finish a long rest.

Your Instinct Die increases in size as you gain levels in this class. It becomes a d6 at 5th level, a d8 at 11th level, and a d10 at 17th level.

Ranger Conclave1st level+
At 1st level, you choose to emulate the ideals and training of a ranger conclave. Your choice grants you features at 1st level, then again at 3rd, 6th, 11th, and 17th level.
Spellcasting1st level+

Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your ranger spells.

Spell save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier

Spell attack modifier = your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier

When you learn a Ranger spell, you can choose any Ranger spell of a level for which you have spell slots. Gaining access to a higher spell level never forces you to learn a spell of that level.

Fighting Style2nd level+
At 2nd level, you adopt a particular style of fighting as your specialty. Choose one of the available Fighting Style options. You can't take a Fighting Style option more than once, even if you later get to choose again.
Know Your Enemy2nd level+

Starting at 2nd level, if you spend at least 1 minute observing or interacting with another creature outside combat, you can learn certain information about its capabilities compared to your own.

Choose two of the following characteristics: Strength score, Dexterity score, Constitution score, Armor Class, current hit points, total class levels if any, or Ranger class levels if any.

The DM tells you whether the creature is your equal, superior, or inferior in regard to each chosen characteristic.

Combat Instincts3rd level+

Whenever you take the Attack action on your turn, your walking speed increases by 10 feet until the end of the turn.

When you hit a creature with a weapon attack made as part of that action, you can expend one Instinct Die to use one of the following Combat Instinct options. You can use only one Combat Instinct option per turn.

Guarded Instinct. Roll your Instinct Die and add the number rolled to the damage of the attack. You also gain a bonus to your AC equal to the number rolled until the start of your next turn.

Sweeping Instinct. Roll your Instinct Die and add the number rolled to the damage of the attack. Another creature of your choice that you can see within 5 feet of you also takes damage equal to the number rolled.

Pursuing Instinct. Roll your Instinct Die and add the number rolled to the damage of the attack. You can also push the target up to 5 feet away from you, plus a number of feet equal to the number rolled on the Instinct Die. You can then immediately use your reaction to move up to your walking speed to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the target.

Deft Explorer3rd level+

Canny. Choose one skill you are proficient in. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make using the chosen skill. You can also speak, read, and write 2 additional languages of your choice.

Roving, 6th level. Your walking speed increases by 5 feet.

Tireless, 10th level. As an action, you can give yourself temporary hit points equal to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier, minimum 1. You can use this action a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus and regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. In addition, whenever you finish a short rest, your exhaustion level, if any, is decreased by 1.

Extra Attack5th level+
You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.
Favored Enemy7th level+

Beginning at 7th level, choose a type of favored enemy. You have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track your favored enemies and on Intelligence checks to recall information about them. You also learn one language associated with them, if applicable.

When you deal damage to a favored enemy, you can add your proficiency bonus to the damage dealt.

You choose one additional favored enemy, as well as an associated language, at 10th, 14th, and 18th level.

Heightened Instinct7th level+
At 7th level, you now regain all expended Instinct Dice when you finish a short or long rest.
Land's Stride9th level+
Starting at 9th level, moving through nonmagical difficult terrain costs you no extra movement. You can also pass through nonmagical plants without being slowed by them and without taking damage from them if they have thorns, spines, or a similar hazard. In addition, you gain proficiency in Wisdom saving throws.
Nature's Veil10th level+

This 10th-level feature replaces the Hide in Plain Sight feature. You gain no benefit from the replaced feature and don't qualify for anything in the game that requires it.

As a bonus action, you can magically become invisible, along with any equipment you are wearing or carrying, until the start of your next turn.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

Explorer13th level+

At 13th level, your experience traveling through difficult and unfamiliar terrain allows you to move through the world with ease.

You gain a climbing speed and a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.

In addition, as a bonus action, you can jump up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. This jump does not require a running start and is additional movement.

Mastered Instincts14th level+
Starting at 14th level, whenever you use a Combat Instinct option, you can roll a d6 and use it instead of expending an Instinct Die.
Vanish15th level+

Starting at 15th level, as a bonus action on your turn, you become invisible until the start of your next turn. Anything you are wearing or carrying is invisible as long as it is on you.

You also can't be tracked by nonmagical means, unless you choose to leave a trail.

Feral Senses18th level+

At 18th level, you gain preternatural senses that help you fight creatures you can't see. When you attack a creature you can't see, your inability to see it doesn't impose disadvantage on your attack rolls against it.

You are also aware of the location of any invisible creature within 30 feet of you, provided that the creature isn't hidden from you and you aren't blinded or deafened.

Foe Slayer20th level+
Starting at 20th level, your Wisdom score increases by 4. Your maximum Wisdom score is now 24. In addition, whenever you roll your Instinct Die, you can treat a roll of 3 or lower as a 3.
