Class Features

Hit Points1st level+
Hit Dice: 1d8 per rogue level
Hit Points at 1st Level: 8 + your Constitution modifier
Hit Points at Higher Levels: 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per rogue level after 1st
Proficiencies1st level+
Armor: Light armor
Weapons: Simple weapons, hand crossbows, longswords, rapiers, shortswords, scimitars, whips
Tools: Thieves' tools
Saving Throws: Dexterity, Intelligence
Skills: Choose four from Acrobatics, Athletics, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Perception, Performance, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth.

You gain Expertise in two skills in which you are proficient.
Rogue Weapon Training1st level+
Your training allows you to make deadly use of light and precise weapons. When you deal damage with a light or finesse weapon, you can use a d8 in place of the weapon's normal damage die. If the weapon already uses a d8 or larger damage die, this feature does not increase it.

This applies to qualifying melee and thrown attacks. Daggers, shortswords, scimitars, and whips use a d8 for you, while rapiers remain d8.
Roguish Archetype1st level+
At 1st level, you choose an archetype that you emulate in the exercise of your rogue abilities. Your archetype choice grants you features at 1st level and then again at 3rd, 9th, 13th, and 17th level.
Thieves' Cant1st level+
During your rogue training, you learned Thieves' Cant, a secret mixture of dialect, jargon, and code that allows you to hide messages in seemingly ordinary conversation. Only another creature that knows Thieves' Cant understands such messages.

You also understand a set of secret signs and symbols used to convey short messages, such as whether an area is dangerous, protected, under observation, or contains a safe refuge.
Cunning Action2nd level+
Your quick thinking and agility allow you to move and act quickly. You can take a bonus action on each of your turns in combat. This action can be used only to take the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.
Sneak Attack2nd level+
Once per turn, you can deal extra damage to one creature you hit with an attack using a finesse or ranged weapon if you have advantage on the attack roll.

You do not need advantage if another enemy of the target is within 5 feet of it, that enemy is not incapacitated, and you do not have disadvantage on the attack roll.

Your Sneak Attack damage is determined by your Rogue level: 2d8 at 2nd level, 3d8 at 5th, 4d8 at 9th, 5d8 at 13th, and 6d8 at 17th.
Steady Aim3rd level+
As a bonus action, you give yourself advantage on your next attack roll during the current turn. You can use this bonus action only if you have not moved during the current turn. After you use it, your speed is 0 until the end of the current turn.
Ability Score Improvement4th level+
You can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. You cannot increase an ability score above 20 using this feature. If feats are allowed, you can choose a feat for which you qualify instead.
Uncanny Dodge5th level+
When an attacker that you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to halve the attack's damage against you.
Follow Up5th level+
When you reduce a hostile creature to 0 hit points with an attack, you can use your bonus action to choose one of the following:

Pursue. Move up to your speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

Quick Throw. Make one attack with a thrown weapon against another creature within the weapon's normal range.

You can use Follow Up only once per turn.
Expertise6th level+
At 6th level, choose two additional skills in which you are proficient. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make that uses either chosen proficiency.
Evasion7th level+
When you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, you instead take no damage on a successful saving throw and half damage on a failed one. You cannot use this feature while incapacitated.
Ability Score Improvement8th level+
You gain an Ability Score Improvement.

Reliable Talent11th level+
Whenever you make an ability check that lets you add your proficiency bonus, you can treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10.
Deadly Precision11th level+
Whenever you deal Sneak Attack damage, add your proficiency bonus to the damage.
Ability Score Improvement12th level+
You gain an Ability Score Improvement.
Blindsense14th level+
If you are able to hear, you are aware of the location of any hidden or invisible creature within 10 feet of you.
Elusive15th level+
No attack roll has advantage against you while you are not incapacitated.
Ability Score Improvement16th level+
You gain an Ability Score Improvement.
Stroke of Luck18th level+
If your attack misses a target within range, you can turn the miss into a hit. Alternatively, if you fail an ability check, you can treat the d20 roll as a 20. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
Ability Score Improvement19th level+

Reliable Strikes20th level+
When you make an attack roll using a finesse or ranged weapon, you can treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10. A natural 1 still misses.
